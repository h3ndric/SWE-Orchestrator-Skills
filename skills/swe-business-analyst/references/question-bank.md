# Question bank

Pick what's relevant. Skip anything already answered or obvious from the research. Each question works best as multiple-choice with a recommendation.

## Vision and goal
- In one sentence, what should this do for people?
- Imagine it's a year from now and it worked. What does that look like? (number of users, revenue, time saved, or "I use it daily")
- Is this for you, for a team or company, or for the public?
- Are we aiming for: A) a quick MVP to test the idea, B) a solid v1 for real users, C) the full product vision, built in stages?

## Users
- Who is the main user? (role, tech comfort, how often they'd use it)
- Are there other user types, such as admins, reviewers or customers of your users?
- How do they solve this problem today? What's annoying about that?
- Where do they use it: desk, phone, on the go, inside another tool (Slack, email, browser)?

## Problems and journeys
- What are the top 3 problems, ranked?
- Walk me through the most important journey: what's the first thing they do, and then?
- What's the "aha" moment, when the user first sees the value?
- What should happen when something goes wrong, like bad input, no results, or the AI being unsure?

## Features (MoSCoW)
- From the research, competitors all have [X, Y, Z]. Which are must-haves for launch?
- [Differentiator] would set us apart. Must, nice-to-have, or skip?
- Is there anything we should explicitly NOT do?

## AI-specific
- What should the AI do exactly: summarize, answer questions, classify, generate, take actions?
- Can you give 2–3 real examples of input and the output you'd love? And one output that would be unacceptable?
- How accurate does it need to be? Is "usually right, user can fix it" OK, or must it be near-perfect?
- Should it cite sources or show its reasoning?
- Can it take actions on its own (send emails, change data), or only suggest them?
- Is it OK for it to take a few seconds, or must it be instant?

## Data and privacy
- What information will the app store? Is any of it sensitive (personal, health, financial, company-confidential)?
- Where does the data come from: user uploads, integrations, public web, APIs?
- Can user data be sent to an AI provider? Any region or compliance needs (GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2)?
- Should users be able to delete their data or export it?

## Accounts and access
- Do people need an account? Sign in with Google, email, or something else?
- Different permission levels (owner, member, viewer)?
- Free, paid (subscription or one-time), or invite-only? Is there a free tier?

## Success and constraints
- How will you measure success? (signups, weekly active users, time saved, revenue)
- Budget: what's OK per month for hosting and AI? Any one-time budget?
- Timeline: is there a date that matters?
- Platform: web app, mobile app, desktop, browser extension, CLI, API?
- Any accounts or tools you already have and want to use (cloud provider, domain, GitHub, Stripe)?
- Languages and regions?

## The MVP cut
- If we could launch with only 3 features, which 3?
- What would make you embarrassed to launch? (That's the minimum quality bar.)
- What can wait until after the first real users try it?
