# Clarity checklist: the interview is done when all items pass

Mark each item ✅, or ⚠️ when the owner has accepted it as an assumption. Any ❌ means ask more.

## Vision
- [ ] The vision fits in two sentences, and the owner agreed with the wording.
- [ ] The target level (MVP / v1 / full vision) is explicit.
- [ ] Success metrics are measurable: a number plus a timeframe.

## Users
- [ ] At least one persona with: who they are, their current workaround, their main frustration.
- [ ] All user types who touch the system are named (including admins).

## Features
- [ ] Every Must feature has: **a user** (persona), **a reason** (problem it solves), **an observable outcome** that could be written as Given/When/Then.
- [ ] The MVP set is explicitly chosen: the owner said "these for launch".
- [ ] Out-of-scope items are listed (at least 2).
- [ ] No feature description contains "TBD", "etc.", "and so on", "something like", "maybe" without resolution.
- [ ] No two answers contradict each other. Examples: "no accounts" alongside "personal history saved"; "free" alongside "$0 budget plus paid AI per request" with no plan for the cost.

## Journeys
- [ ] The primary user journey is written step by step, including what happens on error or empty states.

## AI (if applicable)
- [ ] Each AI capability has 2+ examples of good output and 1+ example of bad output, or the owner said "you decide" and you wrote examples they approved.
- [ ] The accuracy or quality expectation is stated: tolerance level, need for citations, human-in-the-loop or not.
- [ ] Autonomy level is clear: suggest only, or act on its own.

## Data and constraints
- [ ] Data sensitivity and privacy/compliance needs are known.
- [ ] Budget (monthly) and any deadline are known, or explicitly "flexible".
- [ ] Platform(s) are known.
- [ ] Existing accounts and tools to reuse are known.

## Playback
- [ ] The owner confirmed the "Here's what I heard" summary.
